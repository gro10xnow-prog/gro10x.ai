## Overview

Chatbase lets you build an AI agent trained on your own content (your documents, your website, your knowledge base) and put it in front of customers wherever they already are. The AI agent answers questions from what you've taught it, takes actions on your behalf, and hands off to a person when it should.

Building one takes about 10 minutes: create the AI agent and train it, test and refine its answers, then deploy it.
