# WhatsApp

> Connect a WhatsApp number so your AI agent can handle customer conversations on WhatsApp

## Overview

Integrating WhatsApp with Chatbase lets your AI agent communicate directly with customers on WhatsApp, handling inquiries and automating responses. This guide walks you through connecting your AI agent to a WhatsApp phone number.

<Card title="What you can do:" icon="comments">
  Answer customers on the channel they already use, around the clock, with the same AI agent, knowledge, and actions you use on your website.
</Card>

<Info>
  Attachments are supported, so your AI agent can process them and respond based on their content. See [Attachments](/docs/user-guides/chatbot/channels#attachments).
</Info>

<Note>
  You can connect more than one WhatsApp number to your AI agent. Once connected, all your numbers are managed from **Channels → WhatsApp → Manage**.
</Note>

## Before you start

The WhatsApp phone number integrated with the AI agent can only be used by the AI agent. It can't also be used on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The number is already used with WhatsApp">
    Delete the account in the app first:

    1. Open the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app.
    2. Navigate to **Settings > Account**.
    3. Select **Delete my account**. This may take a few minutes, after which the number becomes available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You previously used WhatsApp through Meta Developer for business">
    You must disable **two-step verification**:

    1. Log in to your [WhatsApp Business Account](https://business.facebook.com/wa/manage/home/).
    2. Choose the phone number you'd like to integrate.
    3. Navigate to **Settings > Two-step verification** and turn it off.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Make sure you have an approved display name before integrating with your AI agent. You can [read more about display names](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/338047025165344) on Meta's site.

## Automatic lead collection

Chatbase can automatically collect basic customer information from WhatsApp conversations, including the phone number and name when available.

To enable it:

1. Go to **Channels → WhatsApp → Manage → Leads**.
2. Toggle **Enable Automatic Collection of Leads**.
3. Select the WhatsApp number or numbers you want to apply this to.

Once enabled, Chatbase captures lead details whenever a customer messages your WhatsApp number.

<Note>
  For more advanced lead capture and customization, use the [Collect Leads action](/docs/user-guides/chatbot/actions/collect-leads) in your AI agent.
</Note>

## Messaging limits and billing

Once your integration is active you can send 1000 free messages monthly. To send more, add a payment method in your [Meta billing settings](https://business.facebook.com/billing_hub).

<Info>
  You can enable or disable the WhatsApp integration without disconnecting it. Disabling pauses messages while keeping your setup intact, and you can re-enable at any time. New chats still appear in your Chatbase dashboard chat logs.
</Info>

## Limitations

* **The number is exclusive to the AI agent.** A number connected to Chatbase can't also be used in the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app.
* **1000 free messages per month.** Beyond that, a payment method is required in Meta billing.
* **Free-form replies are limited to a 24-hour window.** After that you must use an approved template. See [WhatsApp Templates](/docs/user-guides/integrations/whatsapp-templates).
