## 2. Archetype Deep-Dives & Squad Dispatch Protocols

### Archetype A: Inbound / Direct Requirement Ingress (The Bespoke Solution Rail)
* **Trigger:** An enterprise client approaches with an explicit operational bottleneck or training brief.
* **Operational Flow:**
  1. **Triage & Asset Ingestion:** Founder drops raw workflow documentation, team rosters, or recorded pain points into Central CoS conversation.
  2. **Solution Architect Dispatch (`[E1]`):** Analyzes workflows, isolates root-cause friction, and authors a bespoke execution blueprint.
  3. **Proposal Engine Packaging (`[E2]`):** Generates an official interactive proposal (`/p/<token>`) featuring commercial investment tiers, clear scope boundaries, and a 50% mobilization advance.
  4. **Verification & Dispatch:** CoS probes endpoint over HTTPS; Founder relays proposal link directly to client decision-makers.

---

### Archetype B: Strategic Partner Deal Origination (The Industry Sandbox Rail)
* **Trigger:** A strategic industry partner (e.g., Arif Ahmed `GRO-PARTNER-01`, Sam `GRO-PARTNER-02`) brings a qualified enterprise or civic opportunity within a specific domain (e.g., EV transit, healthcare ops, core banking integrations).
* **Operational Flow:**
  1. **Domain Boundary Definition:** Central CoS scopes a tight, highly visual prototype brief (realistic scale, authentic localized pricing, bilingual UI).
  2. **Foundry Lab Director Dispatch:** Builds a high-fidelity interactive spatial/telemetry simulator in `public/prototypes/<slug>/` on Port 3000.
  3. **Proposal-Wrapped Prototype Packaging:** Wrapped inside an official proposal (`PROP-YYYY-<PARTNER>`) with mobilization advance terms. Standalone naked prototype links are strictly prohibited (Rule 6.6).
  4. **Early Partner Review:** Delivered to the deal originator 24–48 hours ahead of the board presentation to incorporate feedback.

---

### Archetype C: Consultative Enterprise AI Partnership Pitch (The Co-Venture Rail)
* **Trigger:** Founder targets a high-growth scale-up, former corporate ally, or enterprise flagship (e.g., Shomvob Technologies) to pitch a comprehensive AI transformation, co-branded academy, or proprietary tooling suite.
* **5-Stage Execution Protocol:**

#### Stage 1: Institutional Forensics & Persona Mapping
* Map leadership background (e.g., CEO preferences, CTO technical lineage).
* Identify investor capitalization (e.g., Seed/Series A VCs) and board composition.
* Audit international donor grant portfolios (e.g., Gates Foundation, UNICEF, Swisscontact) to identify non-dilutive co-funding opportunities.
* Uncover active expansion vectors (e.g., international skilled migration, new corporate sectors).

#### Stage 2: Multi-Vector Deep Research & Reserve Brainstorming
* Formulate open-ended deep-research prompts exploring 5 strategic vectors:
  1. *The Donor & Impact Grant Vector* (Vernacular/voice accessibility).
  2. *Enterprise B2B SaaS Vector* (Transforming commissions into recurring SaaS).
  3. *Operational Cost Elimination Vector* (AI phone screeners, verification OCR).
  4. *Fintech & Retention Vector* (Earned wage access, micro-loans).
  5. *Unconventional Moonshots* (Overseas pre-departure visa simulators).

#### Stage 3: Interactive Pitch Deck & Simulator Engineering
* Built by `Foundry Lab Director` at `public/prototypes/<slug>-pitch/index.html`.
* **Visual Identity:** Tailored with the prospect's authentic brand colors and official logo.
* **Human Impact Visuals:** Grounded with real imagery of the target audience (e.g., frontline workers, riders, healthcare staff) rather than generic tech icons.
* **Interactive Dynamic Projections:** Interactive sliders and dynamic SVG charts allowing the prospect's CEO to model ARR, learner volume, and conversion metrics live during the pitch.
* **The "Explore All Ideas" Vault:** A dedicated, full-screen grid of reserve strategic vectors that can be opened on the fly if the discussion pivots.

#### Stage 4: Live Operational Automation Showcase
* Demonstrate operational superiority by running internal GRO10X tools live during the call (e.g., `GRO10X Meet Copilot` transcribing the Google Meet in real time and generating instant minutes).

#### Stage 5: Co-Venture Commercial Structuring
* Pitch high-volume, low-friction entry points:
  * **50/50 Joint-Venture Revenue Share** on co-branded digital products (e.g. AI Academy).
  * **Zero-Capex 30-Day Managed Tooling Pilot** for enterprise intelligence tools (e.g. Demand Scout, TalentSentry).
