▼

▼

Unlike generic chatbot wrappers, a real human systems specialist at GRO10X configures your teammate's boundaries, tunes prompt fidelity, tests edge cases, and delivers your personalized onboarding plan. Your specialist remains available on WhatsApp for custom adjustments.

▼

No. On the Personal B2C Prepaid plan, your credits never expire. You only consume credits when actionable work (document extraction, research brief, fired reminder session) is executed.

Zero new apps, zero browser extensions. Your teammate lives natively as a verified WhatsApp contact. You can send text messages, forward PDF documents, record voice notes, or share photos exactly like messaging a teammate.
