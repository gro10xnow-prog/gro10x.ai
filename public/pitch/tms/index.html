02 / OPERATIONAL TAX

Where High-Scale Field Execution Leaks

When field forces scale across hundreds of territories, manual coordination creates operational friction, audit fatigue, and blind spots.

📸

Manual Merchandising Audit Bottleneck

Human auditors reviewing thousands of shelf photos daily experience severe cognitive fatigue. Duplicate uploads, old gallery photos, and planogram non-compliance slip through undetected.

⏳

Lagged Field Report Consolidation

Daily logs from territory supervisors require heavy manual collation, transcription, and validation—creating reporting lag that stretches across days rather than hours before brand teams see truth.

📉

Invisible Out-of-Stock (OOS) Blindspots

Stockouts, distributor delivery gaps, and competitor display takeovers go unmeasured between periodic audit visits, leaving brand sales leaking silently.

👥

High-Churn Field Force Sourcing

Activation campaigns require sourcing, screening, and scheduling dozens or hundreds of brand promoters under intense campaign deadlines with heavy manual recruiter overhead.

04 / SOLUTION 1

Vision AI: Automated Shelf & Display Verification

Transforming manual photo audits into an automated visual inspection pipeline that catches display errors instantly and prevents audit fraud.

Step 1 · Mobile Upload

Native Field Capture

Merchandisers snap photos of end-caps, shelf bays, and cooler doors via their standard field flow. Metadata (GPS, timestamp) is extracted securely.

Step 2 · Computer Vision

Automated Checks

  • Display Presence: Brand facings & POSM verified
  • Duplicate Detection: Reused gallery photos flagged
  • Geo-Lock: Verified against assigned outlet coordinates
Step 3 · Exception Routing

Supervisor Review

Auditors no longer scroll through thousands of clean photos. Only flagged violations, missing POSMs, or suspected duplicates appear on the supervisor desk.

🎯

Pilot Design Intent: The pilot is engineered to automatically clear the compliant majority of routine shelf submissions, routing only flagged exceptions to human supervisors for high-priority review.
