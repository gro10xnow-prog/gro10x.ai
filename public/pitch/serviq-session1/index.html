💎 The Core Secret

The Master Blueprint for Flawless AI Execution: R-C-T-O

The 4 pillars that turn any prompt into senior-analyst gold in 5 seconds.

R
Role · Who is Speaking?

Senior EOR Lead

Give the AI a persona, seniority level, and brand tone (e.g., "Senior Client Operations Manager at Serviq BPO").

C
Context · What Happened?

Ground Realities

Feed the exact facts: bank clearing maintenance window, payroll files dispatched on Thursday, expected SMS times.

T
Task · What to Produce?

Clear Deliverable

Draft a reassuring client email to HR Director Tanvir Ahmed with an operational hotline for escalations.

O
Output · Guardrails

Strict Constraints

3 paragraphs, under 150 words, bulleted timeline, calm executive empathy, zero generic brackets or placeholders.

💡 Formula Invariant: Role + Context + Task + Output Guardrails = 100% Boardroom-Ready Output. The RCTO Standard
