Mental Model 1: The "Tireless IBA Intern" Metaphor

Imagine that this morning at 9:00 AM, a brilliant new junior executive walked into Serviq’s Banani headquarters. They graduated at the top of their class from IBA or Harvard Business School with a photographic memory: they have read every labor law, dictionary, business textbook, and marketing framework published in human history. They type at 1,000 words per minute and never suffer from afternoon fatigue or caffeine crashes.

However, there is one massive catch: This intern is sitting inside a soundproof glass office. They have never set foot in a Bangladeshi distribution hub. They do not know who Meraz Bhai is, they do not know what Paylio does, and they have no idea that Bangladesh Bank's BEFTN network had scheduled maintenance this morning. Their clipboard is completely blank.

If you open the door, casually toss your head in, and say: "Hey, write an email about delayed salaries," what will happen? Because the intern has zero context, they will guess and produce a cold, robotic template: "Dear Sir or Madam, We regret to inform you..." Sending that to an MNC client causes panic and an angry escalation call to Meraz Bhai.

⚠️ The Golden Law of AI Delegation: AI does not fail because the model is flawed; it fails because the human manager provided an incomplete, lazy delegation.

Page 3: The Universal RCTO Framework & Formula Card

The four foundational pillars separating amateur 4-word requests from executive-grade operational execution.

R
ROLE
Who is speaking? Sets authority & voice.
C
CONTEXT
What happened? Ground facts & client data.
T
TASK
What to produce? Specific deliverable.
O
OUTPUT
Strict guardrails: format, tone, blacklist.
1. R — ROLE (কে কথা বলছে?): Establishes seniority and posture.
• Weak: "You are an assistant."
• Serviq Standard: "You are a Senior Client Operations Manager at Serviq BPO Limited, managing 4,700+ payrolls across Bangladesh."
2. C — CONTEXT (কী ঘটেছে? পুরো পটভূমি): Injects factual ground realities.
• Weak: "Some salaries are late."
• Serviq Standard: "Sunday morning. Client HR Director inquired about 45 field promoters' SMS alerts. Bangladesh Bank BEFTN server maintenance until 1:00 PM. Payroll files dispatched Thursday before cutoff."
3. T — TASK (নির্দিষ্ট করে কী বানাতে হবে?): Clearly specifies the deliverable and business goal.
• Weak: "Write an email to them."
• Serviq Standard: "Draft a reassuring, authoritative email update to HR Director Tanvir Ahmed. Clarify the banking window without sounding defensive, confirm 2:00 PM–4:30 PM reflection, and provide operations hotline."
4. O — OUTPUT GUARDRAILS (কড়া নিয়ম ও ফরম্যাট): Enforces word limits and structure.
• Weak: "Keep it short."
• Serviq Standard: "Max 140 words. Exactly 3 paragraphs. Include a 3-step bulleted timeline. Zero generic placeholders. Calm corporate empathy. Sign off formally from Serviq Operations Desk."
✂️ TEAR-OUT / PIN TO DESKTOP: UNIVERSAL RCTO BUILDER Fill In The Brackets
ROLE: Act as [Exact Job Title, e.g. Senior Operations Lead at Serviq BPO] speaking with [Tone: authoritative / empathetic] posture.
CONTEXT: Organization: Serviq BPO (4,700+ deployed workforce). Situation: [Describe incident, client account, dates]. Raw data: [Paste unformatted notes/roster].
TASK: Generate a [deliverable: email / status report / meeting circular] addressed to [Client HR Director / Field Team] to achieve [reassurance / task signoff].
OUTPUT: (1) Under [X] words, (2) [3 bullet points], (3) [British English / Bangla], (4) Zero placeholders, (5) Sign off as Serviq Operations Command Desk.
