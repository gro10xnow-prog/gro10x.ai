8 Verified Board Objections · 1-Line Bangla Answer + English Supporting Points
⚔️ Objection 1: "Our rates differ from yours"
"Ei shob figure illustrative — pilot shuru holei Week 1-e apnader official schedule onujayi exact rate lock kora hobe."

• All figures in the prototype and deck are illustrative defaults.

• Rates will be strictly calibrated to National Housing's official published schedule during Week 1 setup.

• Zero rates go live without designated management sign-off.

⚔️ Objection 2: "Does the application form work today?"
"Calculator live test kora jacche, ar form routing pilot-er Week 1–4 build; akhon kono customer data capture hocche na."

• The financial calculator is 100% interactive and functional today.

• Form routing to branch loan officers is built in Weeks 1–4 of the pilot.

• Today's prototype is a private sandbox and collects zero customer data.

⚔️ Objection 3: "Where is the chatbot?"
"Ei proposal-e kono chatbot nei — Board chaile future add-on hishebe consider kora jete pare, kintu pilot-e marketing layer-e focus."

• Chatbots and AI conversational agents are not included in this proposal.

• Engagement is focused strictly on modern public web, social media, and branch lead capture.

• An automated assistant can be discussed as a future add-on only if the Board requests it.

⚔️ Objection 4: "What revenue share do you expect?"
"Revenue share rate pilot terms shathe management-er shathe boshe mutually define kora hobe — amader BDT 50,000 fixed fee stands on its own."

• Conversion fee is mutually defined with executive management prior to campaign rollout.

• Tied strictly to verified leads that successfully disburse as loans or term deposits.

• The fixed pilot fee of BDT 50,000/month covers all technical and creative deliverables independently.

⚔️ Objection 5: "Your dossier said October to December"
"Dossier reference cycle chhilo — amader 3-month pilot calendar running FROM SIGN-OFF."

• The October–December dates were an illustrative planning cycle in the written draft.

• The contractual 3-month pilot runs strictly from the date of formal sign-off.

• Full 12 weeks of execution guaranteed regardless of calendar start date.

⚔️ Objection 6: "Why GRO10X?"
"Traditional agency shudhu static banner dey — amra working prototype agei baniye apnader haate tule diyechi, amader ke measured results diye judge korun."

• Traditional ad agencies lack software engineering capability; pure tech firms lack creative discipline.

• The interactive prototype is already built and working in your hands before contract signing.

• Judge the pilot strictly on measurable branch lead flow and conversion ROI.

⚔️ Objection 7: "Data security and core banking"
"Pilot-e kono core banking integration nei, kono internal customer data migration nei, ar protita creative apnader compliance theke approve hobe."

• Operates 100% on the public marketing layer with zero core banking access.

• Zero customer account records or banking credentials are migrated or stored.

• Full compliance with Bangladesh Bank IT guidelines and NBFI standards.

⚔️ Objection 8: "The 'Guaranteed' creative line"
"Dossier-er creative concept sample matro — live public publication-er age protita copy apnader legal & compliance team verify korbe."

• Creative samples in the dossier are concept designs demonstrating visual grading.

• Final customer copy will strictly replace illustrative slogans with compliance-approved phrasing.

• Nothing goes live without formal sign-off from National Housing's legal committee.
