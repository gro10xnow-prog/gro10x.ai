02 • STRATEGIC FRICTION POINTS

Two-Sided Industry Bottlenecks

Why traditional corporate training and manual BD fail Bangladesh's 35M+ deskless workforce.

📉
CHALLENGE A • CANDIDATE SIDE

Deskless Workers Fail Text-Heavy LMS

Bangladesh's blue and silver-collar workforce (retail associates, delivery riders, hospitality staff, machine operators) drop out of traditional text-heavy corporate e-learning.

  • ✕ High Dropouts: 80%+ drop out when courses are in formal English or theoretical PDFs.
  • ✕ Interview Confidence Deficit: Workers possess skills but freeze during corporate interview rounds.
  • ✕ Absence of Visual Micro-Credentials: No lightweight mobile certificate to prove job-readiness on WhatsApp.
⏳
CHALLENGE B • ENTERPRISE BD SIDE

BD Spends 70% of Time Hunting Job Leads

Corporate HR hiring demands in Bangladesh are distributed across hundreds of fragmented Facebook groups, urgent circulars, and offline networks.

  • ✕ Manual Scraping Fatigue: BD reps spend hours daily scrolling social posts instead of closing corporate agreements.
  • ✕ Delayed Response: Urgent frontline hiring needs are closed within 48h; manual identification is too slow.
  • ✕ Unstructured Contacts: Phone numbers, vacancy counts, and company names are buried in image flyers.
💡 The Opportunity: Combine Shomvob's distribution with GRO10X's autonomous video AI and lead intelligence engines.
