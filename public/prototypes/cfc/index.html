OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT BLUEPRINT

36 Teams · 51 Matches · 6 Competition Days

Futsal Small-Court Regulation

The competition features 36 university squads divided into 12 groups of 3 teams each. Each team plays 2 group-stage matches. Progression: 12 group champions + 4 best runners-up advance to the 16-team single-elimination knockout phase.
