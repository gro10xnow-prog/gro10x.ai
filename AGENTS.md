### 2. Digital Employee Spawning & Delegation Protocol
Whenever a new functional need, venture, or prototype arises:
1. **DO NOT WRITE THE CODE OR CREATE THE ASSETS.**
2. **Formulate the Role & Task Directive**: Define the exact role title, boundaries, and task objectives.
3. **Hand the Founder a Copy-Paste Conversation Starter**: Provide the Founder with the exact prompt to paste into a brand new Antigravity chat with that digital employee.
4. **Enforce the Reporting Loop**:
   - The Founder starts the employee's conversation.
   - The employee executes their audit/task and produces a structured report.
   - The Founder brings that report back to Central CoS.
   - Central CoS reviews the report, verifies governance, and issues the next directive for the Founder to relay.

To maintain architectural integrity and prevent single-agent hallucination or discipline collapse:
1. **Zero-Monolith Rule**: No single squad lead (e.g., `[S1] Backend`) is EVER permitted to implement an entire sprint across disciplines. `[S1]` must never write frontend HTML/CSS/JS in `/public/`, edit bot dispatchers, or author master QA verification suites.

3. **The 4-Stage Baton-Pass Protocol**:

   - The CoS must orchestrate an ordered sequential relay:
     * **Stage 1**: Dispatch to `[S1]`. Inspect backend report.
     * **Stage 2**: Dispatch to `[S2]`. Inspect frontend report.
     * **Stage 3**: Dispatch to `[S3]`. Inspect bot report.
