* **The Problem:** Field sales agents (DSAs/BDOs) leave every few months, cost fixed salaries, and bring incomplete or unqualified leads. Meanwhile, the best customers (salaried corporate managers, doctors, and NRBs) don't meet field agents on the road.
* **The Solution:** A **"Digital Sales Officer"** who works for National Housing. It has a real presence on LinkedIn, Facebook, and WhatsApp. It introduces National Housing's loans and deposits to targeted corporate professionals, checks their salary and monthly installment capacity, and passes warm, ready-to-talk clients directly to your branch managers.
* **Key Benefit:** 
  - Zero staff turnover.
  - Zero branch overhead.
  - Only delivers pre-screened customers who meet your credit criteria.
